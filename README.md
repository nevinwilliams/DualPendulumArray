This sketch animates double pendulums.

I based it on a YouTube video by The Coding Train:

	https://youtu.be/uWzPe_S-RVE

where I got most of the math from.  Instead of adding trail-traces
I opted instead to demonstrate the chaotic behaviour of double pendulums and
their sensitivity to initial conditions.  I did this by  animating 
several (5)  at once, with one very small difference to their starting 
parameters. Here, I made  each pendulum's 1st angle vary by one microradian 
from its neighbours.

For illustrative purposes, a beam o laser light raised at an angle of 1 
microrad from horizontal would only have an elevation of 1 mm at a distance of 
1 km:  A very small angle.

This Processing sketch was coded in IntelliJ CE, and moved to Processing 4.4.1.
